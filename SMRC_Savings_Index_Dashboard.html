Executive Summary

This page sets out the findings of the study in eight steps and is the recommended starting point. Each step states a single conclusion, presents the figure supporting it, sets out what it means for the programme, and cites the survey question it derives from so that any point can be verified.

Read in sequence, these eight steps make up the argument of the wave. The remaining pages provide the supporting detail. Selecting a nationality group in the header recalculates the entire page for that group.

Savings Index

The Savings Index condenses saving habit, share of income saved and the outlook for the next year into a single score from 0 to 100 for every respondent. It is built so that the same number can be tracked wave after wave. The weights used here are for illustration, and the live study would use SMRC’s own definition. Selecting a group in any chart recalculates the index for that group.

Headline Numbers By Group

Offer Scenario

Everyone was asked the one thing it would take for them to put part of their savings into a UAE bond or sukuk. Some said they are ready now. Most named a feature: a higher return, a government issuer, a small first ticket, an easy way out, a Sharia-compliant structure, or buying through their own bank.

Tick a feature below and the page counts how many people have just had their stated condition met. Switching several on at once shows how quickly the gains fall away, because each person named only one condition.

One caveat worth holding on to. This is what people told us it would take, not a forecast of what they would actually buy.
